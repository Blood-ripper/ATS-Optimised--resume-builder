        • Mirror the job post's words.If a posting asks for "REST APIs" and "PostgreSQL", use those exact terms wherever they are true for you. Parsers look for the exact words.
          • 
          
        • Use standard section headings.Summary, Education, Skills, Projects, Experience, Certifications, Achievements. Creative headings can hide a whole section from a parser.
          • 
          
        • Keep one column.Tables, text boxes, icons and photos often come out scrambled or missing. This builder uses a single column for that reason.
          • 
          
        • Put real numbers in your bullets.Users, accuracy, response time, marks, rank. Use only figures you can explain in an interview.
          • 
          
        • Start each bullet with an action verb.Built, Designed, Automated, Deployed, Reduced, Analyzed.
          • 
          
        • List only completed certifications.Courses you are still taking or plan to take do not belong on the resume yet.
          • 
          
        • Send a text-based PDF unless the portal asks for Word.Open the ATS view tab to see exactly what a parser reads from your PDF.
          • 
          
        • Keep it to one page.As a student, one tight page beats two thin ones.
          • 
          
        • Tailor it for every application.Pick the target role that matches the posting, reorder your skills, and adjust the summary with Edit text before you download.
          • 
          
        • Name the file clearly.Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf. The download button names it this way for you.
